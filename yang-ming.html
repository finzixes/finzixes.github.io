An international summer experience at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University focused on nanotechnology, materials science, laboratory techniques, and research exposure.

Experience

The program combined lectures, laboratory demonstrations, research presentations, and hands-on exposure to engineering and scientific equipment.

I participated in the program primarily as a student and observer, using the opportunity to learn about research environments and technologies outside of my normal electrical engineering coursework.

Topics & Activities

  • Nanotechnology and materials science lectures
  • Laboratory demonstrations and equipment
  • Microscopy and microfluidics
  • Fabrication and machining exposure
  • CAD and engineering drawings
  • Research presentations and discussions
  • Hands-on experimental demonstrations

Microfluidics

One of the more memorable demonstrations involved a microchannel system used for sperm sorting. I was able to observe the experiment under a microscope and participate by providing a test sample.

This gave me a direct look at how microfluidic structures can be used to manipulate and separate biological samples at very small scales.

What I Took Away

The experience gave me exposure to interdisciplinary research involving engineering, materials science, biology, fabrication, and experimental methods.

It also helped me better understand how engineering concepts are applied in laboratory and research environments outside of traditional classroom projects.
